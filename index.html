Your Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before your visit. Can't find your answer? Ask us directly.

What happens during my first consultation?

Your first visit includes a 30-minute consultation with Dr. Sharma, VISIA® multi-spectral skin analysis, medical history review, and a customized treatment plan with pricing. No treatment is performed during this visit unless you specifically request a same-day procedure (subject to availability).

Are your treatments safe for Indian skin tones (Types III–V)?

Absolutely. Our lasers (PicoSure Pro, Diode, Nd:YAG) are specifically chosen for safety across Fitzpatrick skin types I–VI. Dr. Sharma has extensive experience treating melanin-rich skin and adjusts parameters to minimize PIH (post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation) risk. We also pre-treat with tyrosinase inhibitors when indicated.

How many sessions will I need for laser hair reduction?

Most patients require 6–8 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart for permanent hair reduction. Hormonal areas (face, bikini) may need maintenance sessions annually. Results vary by hair color, thickness, and hormonal status — we'll give you a personalized estimate during consultation.

What is the downtime for CO₂ Fractional Resurfacing?

Expect 5–7 days of social downtime: redness, swelling, and micro-crusting (like fine sandpaper texture). Day 3–5 is peak peeling. You'll need strict sun avoidance, gentle skincare, and post-procedure ointment. Most return to work by day 5–7 with makeup. Full collagen remodeling continues for 3–6 months.

Are injectables painful? Do you use numbing?

We use topical anesthetic (20–30 min) for all filler treatments. Anti-wrinkle injections use ultra-fine 32G needles — most patients describe it as "tiny pinches." Fillers contain lidocaine for comfort. For sensitive areas (lips, tear trough), we offer dental block anesthesia. Pain scores average 2–3/10.

Can I combine multiple treatments in one visit?

Yes, many combinations are synergistic and time-efficient. Common pairings: Laser Toning + HydraFacial, Botox + Fillers (full face), CoolSculpting + EMSCULPT. We'll design a safe sequence during consultation. Some combinations require spacing (e.g., CO₂ laser + injectables: 2-week gap).

Do you offer EMI or financing options?

Yes. We partner with Bajaj Finserv, HDFC, and ICICI for no-cost EMI (3–12 months) on packages above ₹15,000. Instant approval at the clinic with PAN + Aadhaar. Package pricing (15% off) also applies when paid upfront.

What if I'm not satisfied with my results?

We stand by our work. For medical treatments, we offer complimentary follow-up assessments at 2 weeks and 3 months. If a treatment doesn't meet the agreed clinical endpoints (documented via VISIA®), we'll discuss corrective options — which may include additional sessions at reduced or no cost, depending on the situation.
