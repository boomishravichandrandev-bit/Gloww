## Design Context

### Users
**Primary**: Women 25–55 in Anna Nagar/Chennai seeking dermatologist-led aesthetic treatments (acne scars, pigmentation, anti-aging, laser hair removal). **Context**: Mobile-first, often late-night research, high anxiety about skin, evaluating *trust* before booking. **Secondary**: Referring doctors, corporate wellness partners. **Job to be done**: "Convince me this clinic is medical-grade, not a spa — then make booking effortless."

### Brand Personality
**Clinical. Editorial. Unapologetic.**
- **Clinical**: Science-first, dermatologist-led, FDA-cleared, no fluff
- **Editorial**: Curated, high-design, asymmetric, typography-forward — feels like a premium publication
- **Unapologetic**: No generic beauty tropes. No pink/lavender. No rounded everything. Trust earned through design rigor.

**Emotional goals**: Authority → Calm → Confidence → Action

### Aesthetic Direction
**Clinical Editorial** — High-end medical journal meets luxury editorial magazine.
- **Theme**: Light-primary (clinic context = bright, clean, trustworthy). Dark mode as opt-in for evening browsing.
- **Palette**: Deep charcoal (`--ink`), warm sand (`--sand`), surgical teal/cyan accent (`--scalpel`) — OKLCH, perceptually uniform
- **Typography**: **Display** = distinctive geometric/editorial serif or sans. **Body** = refined technical sans. *Selected via font selection procedure (rejecting reflex defaults: Outfit, DM Sans, Figtree, Noto Sans, Inter, Space Grotesk, Syne, etc.)*
- **Layout**: Asymmetric CSS Grid, container queries, fluid `clamp()` scale. No identical card grids.
- **Differentiation**: The *typography* is the hero. Staggered editorial reveal on load. Zero gradient text. Zero side-stripe borders.

### Design Principles
1. **Typography leads** — Hierarchy through scale/weight/space, not color/badges
2. **Asymmetry with intent** — Break the grid to guide eye, not for decoration
3. **Clinical warmth** — Sand neutrals tinted toward brand hue; no sterile grays
4. **One orchestrated moment** — Page load = staged entrance; interactions = subtle feedback
5. **Trust through craft** — Every pixel intentional. No AI-default patterns.
